Our Story

Focused Only on Delhi

Delhi Properties was created to make property search simpler for people looking specifically within Delhi — not Gurgaon, not Noida, not Ghaziabad. Just Delhi.

From Uttam Nagar in the west to Mayur Vihar in the east, and from Rohini in the north to Chhatarpur in the south, our listings span the city's residential and commercial neighbourhoods.

We help people buy, rent and sell flats, builder floors, independent houses, villas, plots, offices and shops — with a simple search experience and quick response on call or WhatsApp.
